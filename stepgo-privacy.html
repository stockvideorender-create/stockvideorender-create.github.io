StepGo

Privacy Policy · Last updated: April 2026

1. Overview

StepGo ("the App") is a free step counter and health tracking app. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects the following data, which stays on your device:

  • Step count, distance, and calories from Apple HealthKit and CoreMotion
  • Water intake tracking (manual entry)
  • BMI data (height and weight, manual entry)
  • Daily goals and preferences

This data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant permission
  • Approximate location (country-level)
  • Device type and OS version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

All ad data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS 14+)

On iOS 14 and later, the App will ask for your permission before accessing your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. HealthKit

The App reads step count, walking/running distance, and active energy burned from Apple HealthKit. This data is used solely to display your activity statistics within the app. We do not store, share, or transmit your health data to any third party.

6. Motion & Fitness

The App uses CoreMotion (pedometer) to provide real-time step counting. This data is processed locally on your device and is not shared externally.

7. Local Storage

The App stores your preferences, goals, achievements, and XP progress locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
