Privacy Policy · Last updated: April 2026

1. Overview

Labirent Koşusu ("the App") is a free maze puzzle game. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect

The App itself does not collect or store any personal data. However, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may collect the following:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID) — only if you grant permission
  • Approximate location (country-level)
  • Device type and OS version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

All ad data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS 14+)

On iOS 14 and later, the App will ask for your permission before accessing your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Local Storage

The App stores only your game progress and best scores locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
