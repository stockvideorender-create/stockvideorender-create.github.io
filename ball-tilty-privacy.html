Ball Tilty – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Ball Tilty ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Ball Tilty itself does not collect any personal information. However, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data to serve relevant ads:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data

Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads in the app. Google's data collection and use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Ball Tilty is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Any data collected by AdMob is subject to Google's retention policies.

Your Choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
